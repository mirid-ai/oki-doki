What’s inside?

Both downloads include the written story, recorded character voices, music and sound effects. An optional local AI model lets you type your own replies in selected conversations; local transcription can put a spoken reply into the text field. You choose whether to use them.

Open AI settings → Set up included AI and microphone, then save for the session. You can also configure the model and transcription separately. Generated replies and transcriptions can be wrong.

A note on this preview

The Linux build was tested on Ubuntu 24.04 x64; its AI and speech runners require glibc 2.34 or newer, libstdc++ GLIBCXX 3.4.30, OpenSSL 3 and libgomp. An earlier Windows build was exercised under Wine; the current route test was inconclusive, and native Windows remains untested. The Windows preview is unsigned and may show a warning. Audio playback was checked, not audibly reviewed.

05 A NOTE FROM THE CREATOR

Made with
a little hope.

A new story, made for people who love getting attached to fictional friends.

Oki Doki began in response to the creator’s recollection of Aviva Sofia ASMR wishing for another Doki Doki Literature Club in her video-game ramble. It’s an original, open-source, unofficial spiritual successor with its own characters and world.

The Last Ticket is a work in progress. Its story and outcomes are written. The game includes spoken lines and local Kokoro voices; optional AI conversations need a compatible service in the browser, or can use the included model in the desktop versions. Generated replies can be inconsistent.

Oki Doki is unaffiliated with Team Salvato. Aviva Sofia ASMR is not involved and has not endorsed this project.
