# Playroom, light-out artwork

The image `playroom-dark.png` was edited from the project's `playroom.png` using the built-in image-generation tool. It retains the original as a separate asset and is used after the playroom light goes out.

Prompt:

> Edit this exact original visual-novel background into its later uncanny state. Preserve the same wide landscape framing, camera position, floor plan, architecture, palette, texture, and positions, shapes and identities of the blanket fort, its three toys, glowing upper arcade, lamplit table, little door, distant corridor and rocking horse. This must be unmistakably the same place before and after, with no new objects and no people. Change only the mood and lighting: the light over the little fort door goes out, the floor lamp has dimmed to a faint ember, and cool violet shadow deepens in the corridor while a thin strip of arcade light still reaches the room. Very subtly let the repeated corridor's perspective feel a little too long, without adding or moving any doors. Keep the beautiful hand-painted anime visual-novel art, high detail and dark but readable textures; eerie and mysterious, not grotesque, with no text or logos. Do not introduce screens, collage, a new lamp or a different room.
